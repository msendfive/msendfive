### 🚀 About Me

I build **mobile applications with Flutter**, integrated with **Google Sheets & Google Apps Script** as a lightweight, low-cost backend alternative to traditional server stacks. My work focuses on internal business tools — inventory & warehouse management, IT asset tracking, and finance tracking — with an emphasis on shipping fast, functional, and reliable systems.

- 🔭 Currently building **internal business tools for GAP** (warehouse & IT asset management systems)
- 🛠️ Comfortable across the full stack: Flutter UI, Apps Script backend, and Google Sheets as a database

- 🌱 Also building personal projects for fun — including a couple's note-sharing app
